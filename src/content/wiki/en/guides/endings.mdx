The Blood of Dawnwalker advertises that its story is shaped by your actions — and nowhere is that more literal than its endings. This guide explains how the ending system of The Blood of Dawnwalker works, which ending has been confirmed at launch, and how to see everything the game has to offer without accidentally locking yourself out. Spoiler policy: structure first, specifics behind clear warnings.

## How Endings Work in The Blood of Dawnwalker

Endings in The Blood of Dawnwalker are not chosen from a final menu. They accumulate. Across the roughly 30-hour story, The Blood of Dawnwalker tracks the big calls you make — who you side with, what secrets you expose or bury, and how you handle the game's central betrayals. By the final act, those accumulated decisions have already narrowed the paths available to you, and the last stretch of quests around **Xanthe** effectively locks your outcome.

The practical consequences of this design:

- **Small choices matter long before they look important.** A dialogue option in Act I of The Blood of Dawnwalker can change who is alive, loyal, or willing to help you in Act III.
- **There is no single point of no return — but the final act is close.** Once you enter the endgame stretch of The Blood of Dawnwalker, save and treat everything as potentially decisive.
- **One playthrough cannot see everything.** The Blood of Dawnwalker is built around replays and save manipulation, and the developers expect you to use manual saves to explore branches.

## Confirmed Endings in The Blood of Dawnwalker

### The Turncoat

The Turncoat is the one fully confirmed named ending of The Blood of Dawnwalker at launch, tied to how you handle the game's themes of loyalty and betrayal. As the name suggests, this ending centers on a defection — whose side you end on, and who you leave behind to get there. Because the route to The Turncoat runs through choices spread across the whole story of The Blood of Dawnwalker, there is no single "do X at Y" instruction that gets you there honestly. The [choices and consequences guide](/guides/choices/) covers the decision categories that feed it.

### Other Endings

The Blood of Dawnwalker has multiple endings beyond The Turncoat — the game's own structure (a dedicated "other endings" cleanup step in completion tracking) confirms more than one alternative outcome. The exact roster and names of every ending in The Blood of Dawnwalker are still being mapped by the community in the launch window; this page updates as they are confirmed, and we would rather say "unconfirmed" than guess.

## How to See Every Ending Efficiently

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports manual saves alongside autosaves, and the intended way to see all endings is save discipline, not three full replays. The standard route completionists use:

1. **Play naturally until the final act.** Enjoy The Blood of Dawnwalker; do not obsess over guides on a first run.
2. **Before the Xanthe endgame stretch, make a dedicated manual save.** Label it mentally as your "ending branch" save. Everything after this point in The Blood of Dawnwalker is where outcomes lock.
3. **Finish the game once, then reload.** Your first ending is whatever your choices earned. Reload the manual save and change the decisive late-game choices to branch to a different ending of The Blood of Dawnwalker.
4. **For remaining branches, reload again.** Most players report that the endings of The Blood of Dawnwalker hinge on a small number of late decisions, so a single well-placed save covers most of the roster.

## Avoiding Missable Content on the Way to the Endings

Two habits protect your endings run in The Blood of Dawnwalker:

- **Save before every major quest, not just the endgame.** Some branches are decided earlier than they appear — the [Brencis fight](/bosses/brencis/) literally changes depending on your choices, with three different versions of that battle in the game.
- **Do side quests before finishing the main story.** Several side threads in The Blood of Dawnwalker feed back into the ending conditions, and some quest givers disappear late in the story.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many endings does The Blood of Dawnwalker have?**
Multiple. The Turncoat is the only fully confirmed name at launch; the community is still mapping the complete roster of Blood of Dawnwalker endings.

**Can I get all endings in one playthrough?**
Effectively yes, with manual saves. The Blood of Dawnwalker is designed so that a save before the final act lets you branch to different endings without replaying the whole game.

**Do choices in side quests affect the ending?**
Some do. The Blood of Dawnwalker tracks more than just main-story decisions, which is why sweeping each settlement for [quest givers](/guides/beginner-guide/) before the endgame is worth the time.

**Is there a best or true ending?**
Rebel Wolves has not designated a canon ending for The Blood of Dawnwalker, and this wiki will not invent one. "Best" is a matter of which characters you want to see survive.
