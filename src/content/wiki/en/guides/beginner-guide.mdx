The Blood of Dawnwalker throws you into a 14th-century Europe torn by war and vampires, and it barely stops to explain itself. This beginner guide covers the things the game assumes you will figure out on your own — the systems that matter most in your first ten hours, in the order you will run into them. Everything here is written for launch version 1.0 of The Blood of Dawnwalker on PC.

## Understand What Kind of Game The Blood of Dawnwalker Is

The Blood of Dawnwalker is an open-world dark fantasy action RPG developed by Rebel Wolves, the studio founded by veterans of The Witcher 3, and published by Bandai Namco. You play as Coen, a man fighting to save his family. The hook that defines the entire game: Coen is **human by day and vampire by night**. The same world plays completely differently depending on the time of day, and learning to plan around that rhythm is the single most important skill in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

It is also a game about consequences. The story of The Blood of Dawnwalker is shaped by your actions and the secrets you uncover — choices you make in hour two can change who stands beside you in hour twenty. That is why the saving advice below matters so much.

## 1. Learn Dodge and Parry Before Anything Else

Combat in The Blood of Dawnwalker is built around timing, not button mashing. Every enemy telegraphs, and the difference between a clean kill and a respawn is reading those animations. Spend your first real fights — the early soldiers and packs near the starting area — deliberately practicing two things: the dodge window and the parry window. Do not rush. Dying to the third bandit in The Blood of Dawnwalker teaches you more than winning a fight you barely understood.

## 2. Make Manual Saves Before Every Major Quest

The Blood of Dawnwalker autosaves, but the structure of the game punishes relying on autosaves alone. Major story decisions can lock or unlock entire quest outcomes, endings, and even who fights beside you. Before you start any main quest with a dialogue-heavy setup, open the menu and make a manual save. The [choices and consequences guide](/guides/choices/) explains why this habit is the single best quality-of-life improvement you can make in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

## 3. Plan Around the Day/Night Cycle

Because Coen is human by day and vampire by night, your capabilities change with the sun. Daytime in The Blood of Dawnwalker favors exploration, talking, and moving through human settlements. Night favors your supernatural side — and the world's supernatural threats. Early on, avoid assuming night is "harder mode." It is a different toolkit. Test both, learn which quests and regions feel safer in daylight, and use the cycle deliberately instead of fighting it.

## 4. Clear the Map Icons as You Go

The open world of The Blood of Dawnwalker is dense with activity markers: towers to visit, monster lairs, soldier camps, bandit camps, and ancient circles. Each one is a short, self-contained combat or exploration challenge that feeds your progression. Rule of thumb: if an icon is on the way to your quest objective, detour and clear it. Skipping all of them is how players end up underleveled for the [boss fights](/bosses/) later in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

## 5. Treat Court Activities as Real Progression

Court activities — challenges like the Gilded Gauntlet, the Lunar Game, and the Ring — look like minigames but they are a genuine progression pillar in The Blood of Dawnwalker. They reward skill points and other resources you need for your build. See the [court activities guide](/guides/court-activities/) for what each one is and which to prioritize.

## 6. Spend Skill Points, Do Not Hoard Them

Leveling gives you skill points, and The Blood of Dawnwalker lets you work toward powerful ultimate perks. New players often sit on ten unspent points waiting for a "perfect build." Do not. A spent point making you stronger this hour beats a theoretical point making you stronger in ten hours. You will understand what to specialize in only after playing.

## 7. Talk to Every Quest Giver

Side quests in The Blood of Dawnwalker are where the world-building and some of its best rewards live. Many are missable if you rush the main story, and several feed back into the main narrative. Make it a habit to sweep each settlement for quest givers before moving on.

## 8. Read the Quest Journal Carefully

The journal in The Blood of Dawnwalker is genuinely useful — objectives update with context, and the [main quest order](/guides/main-quests/) stays readable even when the open world is pulling you in six directions. If you ever feel lost, the answer is almost always in the journal, not the map.

## 9. Turn the Difficulty Down Before You Burn Out

The Blood of Dawnwalker offers difficulty options up to a hard "Duelist" mode. There is no prize for suffering. If a fight stops being fun, lower the difficulty, learn the patterns, and raise it again later. The game is long; finishing it matters more than bragging rights.

## 10. Do Not Ignore the Story's Quiet Moments

Because choices matter so much in The Blood of Dawnwalker, the quiet conversations are where the game hides its real stakes. Pay attention to who asks what of you. The [endings guide](/guides/endings/) exists because small moments turn into big consequences much later.

## The Short Version

If you take only three habits from this Blood of Dawnwalker beginner guide, make them these: practice dodge and parry timing early, keep manual saves before major quests, and clear map icons as you travel. Everything else in The Blood of Dawnwalker builds on that foundation. For what comes after the early game, head to the [main quest order](/guides/main-quests/) or pick a [boss guide](/bosses/) and start preparing.
