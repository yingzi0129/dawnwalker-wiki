Brencis is the most interesting boss encounter in The Blood of Dawnwalker, because it might not be a boss encounter at all — or it might be the hardest one in the game. Depending on the [choices](/guides/choices/) you made earlier in the story, the confrontation with Brencis plays out in one of **three confirmed versions**: fighting Brencis together with Lacra, fighting Brencis together with Crake, or fighting Brencis entirely on your own. This guide explains how the variants work and how to win each one.

## Why This Fight Has Three Versions

The Blood of Dawnwalker tracks loyalty. Across the story, your decisions determine who trusts you enough to stand with you when the confrontation with Brencis arrives — and The Blood of Dawnwalker follows through mechanically rather than just in cutscenes. The Brencis fight is the game's clearest proof that choices rewrite its set pieces, not just its dialogue.

The three confirmed variants:

| Variant | Your Situation | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| With Lacra | Lacra fights at your side | Moderate |
| With Crake | Crake fights at your side | Moderate |
| Alone | No allies — a true solo duel | Hardest |

Which version you get is locked before the fight begins. If you want to know which variant your story has earned, review the [choices and consequences guide](/guides/choices/) — and if you want a different one, this is one of the fights where an earlier manual save genuinely changes the game.

## Universal Strategy: Brencis Himself

Across all three versions of the fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker, Brencis fights like a duelist — closer to [Ambrus](/bosses/ambrus/) than to the crowd-heavy [Bakir arena](/bosses/bakir/). The same core rules apply:

- **Dodge first, punish second.** Brencis's strings are built to punish panic and greed. Take the openings you are given, not the ones you want.
- **Do not trade.** Trading damage with Brencis in The Blood of Dawnwalker loses the exchange almost every time. His damage per opening is higher than yours; the math only works if you fight cleanly.
- **Watch for the escalation.** At low health, Brencis presses harder, like every major duel in The Blood of Dawnwalker. Nothing new is required — the same discipline at a higher tempo.

## Variant Strategy: Fighting With Lacra

With Lacra at your side, the Brencis fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker becomes about coordination rather than survival. Let Lacra draw attention, and use the distraction windows for your real damage rather than racing her to every opening.

Two habits win this version quickly: focus fire when Brencis commits to either of you (a boss dividing attention between two targets takes double the punishment), and do not let yourself get caught between Brencis and the arena wall while Lacra holds his attention elsewhere. This variant is the friendliest introduction to the fight, and most players' stories in The Blood of Dawnwalker route them here or to the Crake version.

## Variant Strategy: Fighting With Crake

Crake fights differently than Lacra — the Crake version of the Brencis encounter in The Blood of Dawnwalker tends to be scrappier and less controlled. Treat it like a brawl you are directing rather than a duel you are sharing: your job is still to be the disciplined half of the pair.

The trap in this version is over-aggression. Crake's style invites you to match the chaos, and Brencis punishes exactly that. Stay the patient one; let Crake be the distraction; keep doing dodge-first combat even when the fight looks winnable early. The Blood of Dawnwalker has ended more than a few Brencis attempts with a sloppy final phase.

## Variant Strategy: Fighting Alone

The solo Brencis fight is The Blood of Dawnwalker at its most demanding: a genuine one-on-one duel with no one to split aggro and no second health pool. If your story has brought you here, prepare the way you would for Ambrus at double intensity:

- Arrive with a fully finished build — every skill point from every [court activity](/guides/court-activities/) spent.
- Stock healing items beyond what feels necessary.
- Practice in the Ring until parry timing feels automatic.
- Most of all: expect a long fight. The solo version of Brencis in The Blood of Dawnwalker is a marathon of patience. Players who rush it lose in the final phase; players who settle in win it.

## Which Version Should You Aim For?

Honest answer: whichever one your story earned. The [endings](/guides/endings/) of The Blood of Dawnwalker are built from the same choices that decide this fight, so engineering a specific Brencis variant in isolation can contradict the ending you actually want. Play the story, save before the confrontation, and if the solo version awaits you, treat it as the game offering its hardest optional exam.

## After the Fight

The Brencis confrontation is a hinge point in The Blood of Dawnwalker — what follows accelerates toward the endgame and the Xanthe storyline. Make a manual save immediately after the fight, then review the [main quest order](/guides/main-quests/) so you know what the final act expects from you.
