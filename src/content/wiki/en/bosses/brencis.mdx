Brencis is the final boss of The Blood of Dawnwalker — but whether you actually fight him, and who stands beside you if you do, is decided hours earlier by [choices](/guides/choices/) you may not have realized you were making. This guide explains how the final quest **A Deal with the Devil** works, what determines each version of the confrontation, and how to win the fight in whatever form your story presents it.

![Brencis the vampire lord with glowing eyes and a clawed hand in The Blood of Dawnwalker](/images/articles/brencis.webp)

## How the Brencis Fight Unlocks

There is no way to rush Brencis. The final quest only opens after the full mid-game:

1. Deal with all three Boyars through their [court activities](/guides/court-activities/) — the [Ambrus](/bosses/ambrus/), [Bakir](/bosses/bakir/), and [Xanthe](/bosses/xanthe/) campaigns — pushing your Infamy to its maximum.
2. Skip time to the next day and watch the Infamy Edict cutscene; Brencis's parley message arrives.
3. **A Deal with the Devil** begins. This is the point of no return — finish everything on the [endgame checklist](/guides/endgame/) first.

One warning before you enter: **any Boyar you left undealt-with reappears as a miniboss inside Greifberg Castle**. Clearing all three campaigns first is not optional advice; it is how you keep the final dungeon to one boss.

## The Four Ways A Deal with the Devil Ends

The final quest is a conversation more than a dungeon, and your dialogue there picks the ending — including whether there is a fight at all. The full endings breakdown lives in the [endings guide](/guides/endings/); what matters for this page:

| Route | What happens | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Turncoat | **No boss fight at all** — you betray your allies and Brencis gives you your family back | Choose 'I just want my family back' then confirm 'I'll do it'; you must have completed The Firebrand, Shadows in the Woods, and Where Loyalty Lies to mark the Manumit hideout |
| The Folk Hero | Fight Brencis **entirely alone** | Choose 'Enough. Time to end this.' with neither ally questline finished |
| The Patricide | Fight Brencis **with Lacra** | Finish her full questline (see the [quest list](/guides/all-quests/)), then speak to her |
| The Knyazmaker | Fight Brencis **with Crake and the Manumit** | Finish the Manumit chain, initiate through Rise at Dawn or Fall Before Dusk |

## Universal Strategy: Brencis Himself

Across all three fight versions, Brencis fights like a duelist — closer to [Ambrus](/bosses/ambrus/) than to the chaotic [Bakir arena](/bosses/bakir/). The same core rules apply:

- **Dodge first, punish second.** Take the openings you are given, not the ones you want.
- **Do not trade.** His damage per opening is higher than yours; the math only works if you fight cleanly.
- **Bank your build.** Arrive with every skill point from every activity chain spent and healing items beyond what feels necessary.
- **Expect an escalation.** At low health Brencis presses harder, like every major duel in the game — the same discipline at a higher tempo.

Detailed phase-by-phase attack data for Brencis is still being community-verified; the fundamentals above are what carry the fight in every version.

## Fighting With Lacra or Crake

With an ally at your side, the fight becomes about coordination rather than survival. Let your partner draw attention, and spend the distraction windows on real damage instead of racing them to every opening. Two habits win these versions quickly:

- **Focus fire when Brencis commits to either of you** — a boss dividing attention between two targets takes double the punishment.
- **Stay the patient one.** Your ally's style invites you to match their aggression, and Brencis punishes exactly that.

Between the two, the practical difference is flavor — Lacra's version and Crake's version play similarly, and both moderate the difficulty well below the solo route.

## Fighting Alone: The Folk Hero Version

The solo Brencis fight is the hardest encounter in The Blood of Dawnwalker: a genuine one-on-one duel with no one to split aggro and no second health pool. If your story brought you here, prepare for a marathon of patience — players who rush lose in the final phase; players who settle in win. And if Duelist difficulty is your goal, know that The Turncoat route skips this fight entirely, which is why most Duelist clears take the betrayal ending instead.

## Which Version Should You Aim For?

Honest answer: whichever one your story earned. The [endings](/guides/endings/) are built from the same choices that decide this fight, so engineering a specific Brencis variant in isolation can contradict the ending you actually want. Play the story, keep your 54 manual-save slots organized, and make one fresh master save before A Deal with the Devil — it is the key to every ending, and every version of this fight.
