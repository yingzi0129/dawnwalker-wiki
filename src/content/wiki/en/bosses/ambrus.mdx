Ambrus is the first of the three Boyars you will face in The Blood of Dawnwalker, and his duel is the game''s first true skill check. The fight arrives as a main quest called **The Gilded Gauntlet** — a formal night duel he demands after you dismantle enough of his operation. No adds, no arena hazards: just you, Ambrus, and everything the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) told you to practice. This guide covers how to unlock the duel, the optional poison plot that can trivialize it, the fight phase by phase, and the choice waiting at the end.

![Coen dueling Ambrus at night in a sword clash in The Blood of Dawnwalker](/images/articles/ambrus.webp)

## How the Ambrus Duel Unlocks: The Gilded Gauntlet

Ambrus is not fought on a story schedule — you earn the duel through his [court activities](/guides/court-activities/). Work through his activity chains (Ambrus''s Prisoners, Collectors, Transports, Dungeons, Cellars, and related quests) until his anger meter — visible on his icon in the Court menu — hits "Angered." Roughly 75% of his activities is enough.

Then the quest appears on the south side of Maragir, and it literally has your name on it: Ambrus has crucified someone at the crossroads with a written challenge nailed to it. He is daring you to a duel to the death, at night, at a location of his choosing. Note the pressure mechanic: the longer you delay after the challenge, the more innocent people die in Maragir. Clearing the fight promptly is the good-guy route — rewards are **500 XP, a skill point, Ambrus''s epic sword, and a legendary heavy chestpiece**.

## Optional: The Bittersweet Toast (Poison Route)

Before the duel, a servant approaches you: Iulian Codrin, who serves Ambrus and knows his weaknesses, wants to talk. Agreeing starts **The Bittersweet Toast**, a side quest in which Iulian plots to poison Ambrus''s drink at the pre-duel toast. Two things matter:

- **Timing.** From the end of The Bittersweet Toast you have three days to show up for the duel on time — and because Ambrus only duels at night, you must ring the bell by two time segments before sunrise on the third day. Miss the window and Iulian''s cover is blown; Ambrus brings a different victim to the toast.
- **The toast dialogue.** When Ambrus proposes the toast, your answer decides how the poison lands. Refusing politely ("Letting a peasant drink first? What kind of boyar are you?" or "I don''t trust anything you offer") makes Ambrus drink from Iulian and be fully poisoned. "Gladly! To a juicy slaughter" gets you and Ambrus both poisoned. "I don''t drink from humans" makes Ambrus gut Iulian on the spot — the poison still reaches him through the blood, but weaker, wearing off partway through the fight. Adding the Tatzelwurm Gland during The Bittersweet Toast saves Iulian from the poison entirely.

A successfully poisoned Ambrus is a trivial Ambrus: he doubles over mid-fight as the magical poison eats his health, and you can literally dodge for the entire duel without swinging once. On Duelist difficulty, this is the intended way to fight him.

## Ambrus''s Attacks: Three Unblockables to Learn

Ambrus has three unblockable attacks, usually paired with a shadowstep that repositions him first. Learn the tells and each one has one clean answer:

- **Spinning overhand.** From a distance he rises into a ready stance, lifts one foot, then shadowsteps toward you and comes down with a spinning overhand slash. **Dodge backwards** as he reappears.
- **Rising slash.** Same ready stance, but no spin — he leans straight into the shadowstep and reappears slicing low-to-high in one line. **Dodge left or right**, then you have a real punish window before his next attack.
- **Figure-8 advance.** From medium or close range he spins his sword in a figure-8 pattern while stepping toward you. **Dodge repeatedly** to keep your distance; if you are extremely close and fast, one claw strike followed by two lateral dodges can out-turn him.

Between unblockables, Ambrus blocks regular attacks adeptly. Your reliable damage comes from **Perfect Block into Perfect Riposte** — and immediately after a riposte, flowing into a claw art attack (with bracers that allow it) is usually the best damage you can get. He shadowsteps away constantly to reset, so keep lock-on engaged so he cannot materialize behind you.

## Phase by Phase

### Phase One: The Three Strings

Before his first health-break, Ambrus cycles the three unblockables above. This is the learning phase: your job is to identify which tell is which and stop swinging at strings that are not finished. One safe hit after a dodge, then reset.

### Phase Two: Scarlet Shield

Past the first break point, Ambrus starts using his signature **Scarlet Shield** ability. You will see a diamond-shaped shield indicator under his health bar and the bar itself turns grey. The trap: **striking him in this state heals him**. Do not attack at all — keep dodging until the shield expires, then resume the riposte routine.

### Phase Three: Blood-Red Flurry

Past the second break point he glows blood red and begins teleporting around you in multi-angle flurries, sometimes appearing in two places at once. This is a defense-only phase: concentrate on avoiding damage until he settles back into his normal strings. Nothing here is new mechanically — the same dodges, at a higher tempo, with zero greed.

## After the Duel: Drain Ambrus or Kill Him — and "Letting Him In"

When Ambrus''s health is nearly gone, a cinematic pauses the fight for a choice: **drain his blood or kill him outright**.

- **Kill him:** clean, done, quest complete.
- **Drain Ambrus:** you gain his Scarlet Shield ability (or +1 to it, if you had already found a vial of his blood), and drinking straight from the source projects you into the vrakhiri afterlife realm — earning the Realmwalker achievement if this is your first visit. Then Ambrus does something unexpected: he begs. A part of his soul can live on inside Coen instead of being devoured by the horrors of the vrakhiri hell — he asks you to **invite him in**.

There is no documented downside to letting Ambrus in. A sliver of his soul rides along in your head: he occasionally comments as you explore the world, and he assists you later in the fight against [Brencis](/bosses/brencis/). It changes no endings either way. Most players drain and accept — the ability alone is worth it, and the commentary is one of the best bits of writing in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should you let Ambrus in?**
Yes, in practical terms. You keep his Scarlet Shield ability either way, inviting him in adds his voice to your journey and help in the Brencis fight, and no negative consequence has been found. If you roleplay a Coen who wants nothing to do with him, refusing works too — the endings do not change.

**What happens if you drain Ambrus?**
You learn the Scarlet Shield ability (the same one he used against you in phase two), visit the vrakhiri afterlife realm once, and get the choice to house his soul. The 500 XP, skill point, sword, and chestpiece rewards are the same as killing him.

**Can you poison Ambrus before the duel?**
Yes — that is the entire point of The Bittersweet Toast side quest. A fully poisoned Ambrus loses health on his own; you can win by dodging alone. It is the recommended approach on Duelist difficulty.

**Why is Ambrus healing when I hit him?**
That is Scarlet Shield, active in his second phase. The diamond shield icon under his health bar means every strike restores his health. Stop attacking and dodge until the shield drops.

**When does the Ambrus duel happen?**
At night only. After the challenge, ring the bell at the duel site to summon him, which costs two time segments. If you arrive before sundown, the game offers to wait for nightfall — spend that hour on something useful instead.
