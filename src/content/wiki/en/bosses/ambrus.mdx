Ambrus is the first true skill check of The Blood of Dawnwalker. Fought as a formal **duel** during the Act II storyline, this is the fight where the game stops testing your build and starts testing you. No adds, no arena hazards, no gimmick — just you, Ambrus, and everything you were supposed to learn about dodge and parry timing in the first act. This guide covers how the Ambrus fight works in The Blood of Dawnwalker and the strategy that gets you through it.

![Coen dueling Ambrus at night in a sword clash in The Blood of Dawnwalker](/images/articles/ambrus.webp)

## Why the Ambrus Duel Is Different

Most combat in The Blood of Dawnwalker lets you compensate with preparation: out-level the encounter, bring consumables, pick favorable engagements. The Ambrus duel strips that away. The fight is deliberately framed as a contest of honor, and mechanically it behaves like one — a contained arena, a single opponent with a deep moveset, and long windows where the correct action is nothing at all.

That last part is what makes Ambrus the wall he is. Players lose this fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker by attacking into strings that are not finished. The duel rewards patience more than aggression.

## Before the Fight: Preparation Checklist

The duel with Ambrus comes at the end of the Act II storyline — you will see it coming. Use the warning:

- **Spend every hoarded skill point.** There is no reason to enter the Ambrus fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker sitting on unspent progression. If you have been saving points "for later," this is later. Our [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) covers why hoarding is a trap.
- **Clear the Gilded Gauntlet first.** If you have reached the court stretch of Act II, the [Gilded Gauntlet](/guides/court-activities/) should already be done — its skill points and gear are exactly what this duel expects.
- **Arrive fully stocked.** Healing items, repair supplies if your gear uses durability, and a fresh manual save. Losing to Ambrus and replaying a long story setup is the worst outcome in The Blood of Dawnwalker.
- **Warm up in the Ring.** If the duel arena is available before the story fight, spend ten minutes there recalibrating your parry timing.

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

The Ambrus duel in The Blood of Dawnwalker follows a classic three-stage structure. Exact timings are still being documented by the community in the launch window, so treat this as the strategic skeleton and watch for the tells described.

### Opening Phase: Feel Out the Strings

Ambrus opens aggressively. His early strings in The Blood of Dawnwalker are designed to punish the instinct to trade damage. For the first phase, your job is almost purely informational: identify his two or three most common opening attacks, learn which ones can be parried and which must be dodged, and resist every urge to combo beyond a single safe hit.

**Key discipline:** after a successful dodge, take one hit, then reset to neutral. Ambrus punishes greed harder than any early boss in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

### Middle Phase: The Punish Window

Once you have the patterns down, the duel becomes about a specific skill: punishing recoveries. Ambrus's longer strings end with visible recovery animations — that is your entire offense. In this phase of the fight, The Blood of Dawnwalker is asking a simple question: can you recognize when a string is over?

If you find yourself getting hit mid-attack, you are swinging at strings that were not finished. Back off, watch two full attack cycles without attacking at all, and only then resume punishing.

### Final Phase: Pressure and Panic

At low health, Ambrus escalates: faster strings, more aggression, longer combos. This is where most failed attempts in The Blood of Dawnwalker fall apart — players who were winning on patience start racing a fight that has not changed its rules. Nothing about the final phase requires new skills. It requires the same patience at a higher tempo. Breathe, keep dodging first and attacking second, and the duel ends.

## Common Mistakes

- **Attacking first.** In the Ambrus duel, the player who swings first usually loses the exchange. Let Ambrus commit, then answer.
- **Panic-rolling.** Chained dodges drain your stamina and leave you standing when his real attack lands. Dodge on the tell, not on fear.
- **Under-preparing.** Walking into this duel under-leveled makes an honest skill fight dishonest. The [main quest order](/guides/main-quests/) page exists so you can check whether you skipped the progression the fight assumes.

## What You Get for Winning

Beyond the story progress, the Ambrus duel in The Blood of Dawnwalker is the gate into the court stretch of Act II — including the [Gilded Gauntlet](/guides/court-activities/) and the road to the Bakir storyline. Beating him also means you have genuinely learned the game's combat, which pays off in every fight that follows, from the [Beast of Balaur](/bosses/beast-of-balaur/) to the endgame.

## After the Duel

When Ambrus falls, take the win and save. The story of The Blood of Dawnwalker accelerates hard from here — the [choices](/guides/choices/) you make in the coming stretch start deciding which [ending](/guides/endings/) you can reach. A manual save after this fight is one of the best-placed saves in the entire game.
