Where the [Ambrus duel](/bosses/ambrus/) is a pure test of one-on-one discipline, Bakir is the opposite: a boss fight with an audience. Fought **during the games** in Act II of The Blood of Dawnwalker, the Bakir encounter surrounds you with interference and tests skills the duel never touched — positioning, target switching, and staying calm when the arena is chaos. This guide covers how the Bakir fight works in The Blood of Dawnwalker and how to prepare for it.

## The Fight's Defining Trait: Crowd Pressure

The games around Bakir are a public spectacle, and the encounter design of The Blood of Dawnwalker leans into that. Unlike the contained Ambrus duel, the Bakir arena gives you secondary threats to manage — other combatants and arena interference that punish tunnel vision. Players who approach Bakir like a second duel, ignoring everything except the boss's health bar, get worn down by the things they were not watching.

The rule for this fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker: **clear the interference on a schedule, not in a panic.** Decide that whenever the arena gets crowded, you spend a few seconds cleaning it before re-engaging Bakir. Sticking to that rhythm beats reacting to crowds after they have already cost you half your health.

## Before the Fight

The Bakir storyline sits at the end of the Act II court stretch in The Blood of Dawnwalker, after the [Lunar Game and the Ring](/guides/court-activities/). By then you should have:

- **Spent court activity rewards.** The skill points from the Gauntlet and the Ring exist precisely so you arrive at Bakir with a finished build, not a theoretical one.
- **A mobility-focused loadout.** Arena fights in The Blood of Dawnwalker reward movement over raw defense — you will be repositioning constantly.
- **A manual save before the quest.** The Bakir quest is story-heavy going in, and you do not want to replay its setup after a loss.

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

Exact attack timings for Bakir are still being mapped by the community during the launch window of The Blood of Dawnwalker; the strategic structure below is what carries the fight.

### Opening: Establish the Edges

When the fight starts, do not charge Bakir. Move to the arena's edge and let the encounter come to you. Fighting at the edges in The Blood of Dawnwalker does two things: it keeps the crowd between you and the far side visible, and it prevents you from getting surrounded — the fastest way to lose this fight is to be in the middle when everything converges.

### Middle: The Two-Target Rhythm

Settle into an alternating rhythm: engage Bakir until the interference builds up, disengage to clear it, re-engage. While on Bakir, apply the same patience the [Ambrus guide](/bosses/ambrus/) preaches — dodge first, punish recoveries only. While clearing interference, be fast and wasteful: burn resources on the small fry if it keeps the arena clean, because Bakir punishes divided attention more than any enemy in The Blood of Dawnwalker.

### Final Phase: Ignore the Spectacle

At low health, the fight gets louder — Bakir presses harder and the arena pressure peaks. This is a mental phase more than a mechanical one. The win condition does not change: clean arena, dodge Bakir's heavies, punish the recoveries. Most losses to Bakir in The Blood of Dawnwalker happen because the rising noise tricks players into abandoning their rhythm. Keep the rhythm.

## Bakir's Attacks: How to Read Them

Without inventing frame data, the attacks Bakir throws in The Blood of Dawnwalker fall into three families, and each has one correct answer:

- **Heavy committed swings** — long windups, big damage. These are your punish windows: dodge the impact, answer during recovery.
- **Quick jabs and shoves** — short strings meant to interrupt you. Do not try to punish these; block or dodge and reset.
- **Crowd-calling moments** — when Bakir plays to the arena, interference spikes. Use these breaths to clear the field, not to race damage.

## Common Mistakes

- **Tunnel vision on Bakir.** The arena punishes it literally every time. The crowd is a mechanic, not background.
- **Fighting in the center.** Center control is Bakir's game. The edges are yours.
- **Bringing a duel mindset.** Everything that won the Ambrus fight still applies here, but it is necessary, not sufficient — Bakir adds a second layer that The Blood of Dawnwalker will keep testing in later fights.

## What Comes After

Beating Bakir closes the Act II court stretch of The Blood of Dawnwalker and pushes you toward the final act — where the [choices](/guides/choices/) you have been making start locking in your [ending](/guides/endings/). Save after the fight. If Bakir gave you trouble, consider reviewing the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) fundamentals before the endgame: the final stretch of The Blood of Dawnwalker assumes everything this fight tested, and then some.
