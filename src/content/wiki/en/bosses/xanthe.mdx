Xanthe is the third of the three Boyars in The Blood of Dawnwalker and the one whose boss quest breaks the pattern set by [Ambrus](/bosses/ambrus/) and [Bakir](/bosses/bakir/). Her duel, **The Cycle of Love**, can be unlocked the same way as the others — by working through her [court activities](/guides/court-activities/) until her anger peaks — but what makes Xanthe unique is that the duel itself is optional. You can kill her, or you can make a deal with her. This guide covers both routes and what each one changes.

![Coen facing the vampire court in The Blood of Dawnwalker](/images/articles/xanthe.webp)

## Who Xanthe Is

The three Boyars each run a different slice of the vampire court's power. Ambrus rules through force and formal duels; Bakir, the Mad Khan, turns cruelty into games; Xanthe's domain is seduction and manipulation — her operation runs on recruiters, abducted girls, artefact transports, and gargoyle manufacture rather than brute patrols. That personality carries into her boss quest: where Ambrus demands a duel and Bakir dies inside his own game, Xanthe is willing to talk.

Her court activity chains include **Blood Guard Recruiters**, **Ancient Digsites**, **Abducted Girls**, **Artefact Transports**, **Blood Guard Mentors**, and **Gargoyle Manufacture**, plus the memorable **Home Sweet Home** activity, which traps you in a looping dream at the top of Silts Tower (escaping it the right way even nets you the legendary Astrologer's Ring — see the [endings guide](/guides/endings/) for how that loop works).

## How to Unlock The Cycle of Love

The unlock works exactly like the other two Boyars:

1. Open the Court menu and complete Xanthe's activity chains across the regions — roughly three-quarters of them is enough to push her anger to the maximum, the same threshold at which [Ambrus cracks](/bosses/ambrus/).
2. When her anger meter maxes out, **The Cycle of Love** appears as a main quest.
3. Save before starting it. This is one of the decisions in The Blood of Dawnwalker that genuinely deserves a fresh manual save, because the choice at the end is permanent for that run.

Each court activity costs time segments — most cost 3 of the day's 16 — so budget your evenings. The 30-day family limit is generous if you cluster her activities by region instead of criss-crossing the map.

## The Choice: Kill Xanthe or Make a Deal

At the climax of The Cycle of Love, Xanthe does something the other Boyars never do: she offers terms. You can go through with the duel and kill her, or accept her deal and walk away.

- **Kill Xanthe:** the threat is removed permanently, the same as Ambrus and Bakir. Clean, complete, and irreversible.
- **Deal with Xanthe:** she survives, and — this is the important part — **she repays the deal during the final assault on Greifberg Castle**, changing the final dungeon in your favor. The [main quest walkthrough](/guides/main-quests/) flags this as one of the decisions that reshapes the endgame.

Neither route locks you out of any [ending](/guides/endings/): the ending is decided in the final quest's dialogue and by the two ally questlines, not by Xanthe's fate. The deal is simply the stronger strategic play — an extra ally inside Greifberg Castle is worth more than the satisfaction of a third duel.

## Fight Tips If You Choose to Duel

If you do fight Xanthe, treat the duel with the same discipline as the other Boyar duels: dodge first, punish second, never trade hits, and arrive with every skill point from her activity chains already spent. Detailed phase-by-phase breakdowns of her attack strings are still being community-verified — check back after the first major speedrun resources settle. The fundamentals that carry the [Ambrus duel](/bosses/ambrus/) — Perfect Blocks, patience through unblockable strings, and zero greed — are the right foundation here.

## After The Cycle of Love

With Xanthe resolved — one way or the other — all three Boyars are dealt with. Your Infamy hits its maximum, the Edict cutscene plays, and Brencis summons you for the final quest, **A Deal with the Devil**. Before you answer that summons, run the [endgame checklist](/guides/endgame/): finish Lacra's and Crake's questlines, spend your skill points, and make your master save. The [Brencis boss guide](/bosses/brencis/) explains exactly what the final fight looks like with each possible ally at your side.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you skip the Xanthe fight entirely?**
Yes — making a deal with her during The Cycle of Love counts as neutralizing her threat, no duel required. She is the only Boyar with a peaceful resolution.

**Does sparing Xanthe change the ending?**
No. Endings are decided by the final quest's dialogue and the Lacra/Crake questlines. Her deal changes the final assault on Greifberg Castle, not the ending menu.

**What is the best order for the three Boyars?**
Any order works. PowerPyx's walkthrough suggests Xanthe around level 25+, after Ambrus (level 1+) and Bakir (level 20+), but the game never forces a sequence.
