The Beast of Balaur is what happens when The Blood of Dawnwalker stops testing your dueling technique and starts testing your nerve. This is a monster hunt in the classic sense — its own questline, its own corner of the world, and a creature fight that plays nothing like the human duels elsewhere in The Blood of Dawnwalker. If [Ambrus](/bosses/ambrus/) was an exam in patience and [Bakir](/bosses/bakir/) was an exam in awareness, the Beast of Balaur is an exam in positioning.

![The Beast of Balaur, a giant winged monster, attacking Coen in a mountain village in The Blood of Dawnwalker](/images/articles/beast-of-balaur.webp)

## Finding the Beast

The Beast of Balaur is tied to a dedicated questline rather than the main story — one of the major hunts in The Blood of Dawnwalker that the world points you toward through its side content. Expect the road to the fight to run through a dangerous region with its own local threats; treat the approach as part of the encounter. Arriving at the Beast's arena half-drained because you rushed through its territory is the most common way this fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker goes wrong before it starts.

Clear the surrounding region first, restock, and only then take the hunt's final step. Our [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) advice about clearing map icons as you travel exists for exactly this situation.

## How the Fight Plays

Creature fights in The Blood of Dawnwalker follow a different grammar than humanoid duels, and the Beast of Balaur is the clearest example:

- **Wide, telegraphed attacks.** The Beast's sweeps and lunges are big, slow to start, and devastating to stand in. These are dodge-only attacks — do not look for parry windows that creature animations are not designed to give you.
- **Punishing greed at close range.** Staying glued to a monster in The Blood of Dawnwalker works until it doesn't; the Beast's close-range counters are built to end that habit. Fight at mid-range, dart in for confirmed openings, and retreat before the retaliation.
- **Environmental awareness.** The arena for this hunt in The Blood of Dawnwalker rewards using the space — obstacles and elevation break charges and buy breathing room. A player who keeps moving the fight around the arena survives; one who plants their feet gets cornered.

## Phase-by-Phase Strategy

Exact attack data is still being documented by the community during the launch window of The Blood of Dawnwalker, but the encounter's structure is consistent with the game's other large hunts:

**Opening:** spend the first minute purely learning the Beast's tells. In The Blood of Dawnwalker, creature telegraphs are long and honest — the deaths come from impatience, not from unreadable attacks. Identify the lunge, the sweep, and the pause that follows each.

**Middle:** settle into the mid-range rhythm. Punish the recovery after lunges, never after sweeps, and keep the arena's usable space behind you rather than letting the Beast back you into a corner. Resource discipline matters here more than in any duel in The Blood of Dawnwalker — the fight is long, and healing items spent early are healing items missing later.

**Final phase:** when the Beast is wounded, it escalates — faster strings, more relentless pressure. As with every major fight in The Blood of Dawnwalker, the last phase asks for the same discipline at higher speed. Hold your spacing, dodge the committed attacks, and let the openings come to you.

## Preparation Checklist

- **Best available gear, fully repaired.** A monster hunt in The Blood of Dawnwalker is a gear check in a way the duels are not.
- **Healing items at maximum stock.** This is a longer fight than the Act II duels.
- **Skill points spent.** If the [court activities](/guides/court-activities/) of your current act are unfinished, finish them first — the progression gap shows most in fights like this one.
- **A manual save before the final step of the hunt.** Standard practice in The Blood of Dawnwalker, and doubly so for optional bosses with long approaches.

## Where This Fits in Your Run

The Beast of Balaur hunt is one of the content bridges in The Blood of Dawnwalker — substantial enough to feel like an event, optional enough to tackle when your build is ready. Players who clear the major hunts of each act in The Blood of Dawnwalker consistently report smoother main-story progress, because the hunts pay out the progression the story then assumes you have. If the final act is pushing back harder than expected, hunts like this one are usually the answer.

## After the Hunt

Restock, save, and check the [main quest order](/guides/main-quests/) — if the Beast of Balaur was your last major side thread, the endgame of The Blood of Dawnwalker is likely waiting. Before you enter the final act, review the [endings guide](/guides/endings/) and make sure your manual-save discipline is in place. The last stretch of The Blood of Dawnwalker rewards exactly the habits this hunt taught you: patience, positioning, and preparation.
